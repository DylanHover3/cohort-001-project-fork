# Cadence

A course platform where learners buy courses from instructors and can ask for their money back.

## Language

### Refunds

**Refund Request**:
A learner's ask to get their money back for a Purchase. It is Requested until someone reviews it, then becomes Approved or Declined. An Approved Refund Request returns the Refundable Amount and ends the learner's access to the course.
_Avoid_: Reversal, Chargeback, Cancellation, Refund (on its own)

**Requested** / **Approved** / **Declined**:
The only states a Refund Request can be in. A Refund Request is open while it is Requested.
_Avoid_: Pending, waiting, raised

**Refund Window**:
The period after a Purchase, as determined, during which a learner can make a Refund Request.
_Avoid_: Cancel window, cancellation period

**Refundable Amount**:
The amount the learner actually paid for the Purchase, which is what an Approved Refund Request gives back.
_Avoid_: List price, course price
